## Commit messages (example)

Write a short subject line in the imperative, then a body that says why the change was made.
Keep each commit to one change.

## Reporting back (example)

When you finish a task, start with what changed and what is left to do. List the commands you
ran to check it, and say plainly if any of them failed.

## Asking first (example)

Ask before deleting files, rewriting history, or running anything that reaches another machine.
